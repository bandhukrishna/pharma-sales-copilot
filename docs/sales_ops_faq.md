# Sales Operations – Frequently Asked Questions

**Document owner:** Sales Operations | **Last updated:** 1 September 2026

**Q: What is our financial year?**
A: The financial year runs from **April to March**. For example, FY27 is April 2026 to March 2027. Quarters are: Q1 = Apr–Jun, Q2 = Jul–Sep, Q3 = Oct–Dec, Q4 = Jan–Mar.

**Q: When someone says "sales", which number should be used?**
A: Unless stated otherwise, "sales" means **net sales in INR** (after discounts). Units are reported separately.

**Q: Which regions do we report on?**
A: Four regions: North, South, East and West.

**Q: How often is sales data refreshed?**
A: Monthly. Data for a month is available by the 10th of the following month.

**Q: Which products are in the portfolio?**
A: Cardivex (10mg and 20mg), Respira Inhaler, Glucora 500mg and Neurolyn 50mg. Neurolyn was launched in July 2025, so it has no sales history before that.

**Q: Who do I contact about a data issue?**
A: Raise a ticket with the Sales Operations data desk and include the report name, month and region.
