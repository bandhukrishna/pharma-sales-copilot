# Field Force Incentive Policy – FY27 (April 2026 – March 2027)

**Document owner:** Sales Operations | **Effective date:** 1 April 2026 | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how quarterly incentives are calculated and paid to field-force colleagues for FY27.

## 2. Eligibility
- Medical Representatives (MRs) and Area Business Managers (ABMs) on the active payroll on the last day of the quarter are eligible.
- A colleague who joins **mid-quarter** is eligible for a **pro-rated** payout only if they joined **at least 45 calendar days before the quarter end**. Colleagues who joined later than this become eligible from the next quarter.
- Colleagues on a performance improvement plan for the full quarter are not eligible.
- Colleagues who resign before the payout date forfeit the incentive for that quarter.

## 3. Measurement basis
- Achievement is measured on **net sales (INR)** at the **region** level for the brand.
- Achievement % = Actual net sales ÷ Target net sales × 100.

## 5. Payout bands
| Achievement | Payout (% of target incentive) |
|---|---|
| 110% and above | 125% |
| 100% to below 110% | 100% |
| 95% to below 100% | 50% |
| Below 95% | 0% |

## 6. Payout timing
Quarterly incentives are paid with the salary of the second month after quarter end (for Q1 FY27: August 2026).
