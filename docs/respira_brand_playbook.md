# Respira Inhaler – Brand Playbook (FY27)

**Document owner:** Respiratory Brand Team | **Effective date:** 1 April 2026

## 1. Brand positioning
Respira is positioned as the easy-to-use maintenance inhaler for adults with persistent asthma who struggle with inhaler technique.

## 2. Target customers
- Primary: Pulmonologists and chest physicians in metro and Tier-1 cities.
- Secondary: General practitioners with high asthma patient volumes.

## 3. Approved key messages
1. **Simple technique:** Respira's breath-actuated design reduces common inhaler-technique errors.
2. **Once-daily dosing:** A single daily dose supports better adherence.
3. **Patient support:** The "Breathe Easy" programme offers free inhaler-technique training for patients through partner pharmacies.

## 4. Messages that must NOT be used
- Do not claim use in children under 12 years. Respira is approved for adults and adolescents aged 12 and above only.
- Do not claim superiority over any named competitor product.
- Do not describe Respira as a reliever or rescue inhaler.

## 5. Seasonal focus
Asthma-related consultations typically rise during the monsoon and early winter. Field teams should plan more Respira detailing between July and November.
