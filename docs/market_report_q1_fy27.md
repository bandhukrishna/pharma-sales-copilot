# Quarterly Market & Competitor Report – Q1 FY27 (April–June 2026)

**Prepared by:** Commercial Insights | **Published:** 20 July 2026

## 1. Overall summary
The portfolio grew modestly in Q1 FY27 versus Q1 FY26. Cardivex and Respira grew, Glucora declined, and Neurolyn continued its strong post-launch uptake.

## 2. Cardivex (Cardiology)
- **Key threat:** Two domestic manufacturers launched a lower-priced competitor, "Cardosart", in May 2026, priced roughly 30% below Cardivex.
- The impact has been strongest in the **West region**, where Cardosart secured early listings with large hospital chains.
- Recommended response: focus on the 20mg strength with specialists and strengthen hospital formulary engagement.

## 3. Glucora (Diabetes)
- Glucora continues to lose share as physicians move newly diagnosed patients to once-weekly injectable therapies.
- Price-led discounting by generic metformin brands is adding pressure in the South and East.

## 4. Respira (Respiratory)
- Performance was broadly in line with seasonal expectations; demand is expected to rise from July with the monsoon.
- No major new competitor entries this quarter.

## 5. Neurolyn (Neurology)
- Neurolyn launched in July 2025 and continues to add new prescribers each month.
- Early feedback from neurologists highlights good tolerability.
